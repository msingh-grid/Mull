Why this fits "trustworthy instrument" and how the other appearance resolves

Rationale

Signal equipment earns trust by refusing to perform: a lamp is lit or it isn't, a level reads what the mic hears, a log records what changed. This direction gives every AI behavior that same physical honesty — the REC lamp, the segmented meter, and the del/ins wiring log make Mull's machinery inspectable at a glance, which is the product's whole differentiator.

The stencil register keeps labels out of the data's way: uppercase tracked mono for classification, plain sentence-case mono for the user's own words. Menu-bar glyph: the same square-bezel lamp as a template glyph — off / pulsing / sweep / amber-attention.

Light mode resolution

The HUD stays appearance-invariant — always this dark panel, an instrument that reads the same over any wallpaper — while the journal, settings, and onboarding flip to a print substrate: #F4F4F0 unbleached paper, carbon-ink text, the same four semantic hues darkened one step for contrast, grid lines going from phosphor-dark to ruled-form gray.
