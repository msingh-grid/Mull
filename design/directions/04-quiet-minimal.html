Two families, five sizes, no display face. The system stack is the one typeface already proven at 11px on non-retina panels; caps gain +0.08em tracking as they shrink, everything else runs untracked. Weight (400 → 600) does all hierarchy work below 15px.

Why this fits trustworthy instrument

An instrument earns trust by adding nothing: this HUD speaks in the operating system's own typographic voice, so it reads as part of the machine rather than a guest performing on top of it. Every hue on screen is a verifiable fact — removed, added, warned, remembered — which means the moment color appears, the eye already knows it carries information, and the 140–200ms fades report state changes instead of animating them. The one filled element in the entire interface is Apply, and its undo path is typeset beside it, so the most consequential pixel is also the most honest one.

Dark mode resolves by inversion, not redesign: paper and ink swap (#ECECEA on a #1E1E1C panel), hairlines become white at 12%, the four semantic hues lift roughly 15% in lightness to hold 4.5:1, and the HUD's shadow deepens with a brighter 0.5px ring so the panel stays separable from dark wallpapers.
