## 2 · Decisions I drove

- **D23 · Interview senior decision-makers, not interns** (13 Sep).
  - Saaketh first suggested interns. I argued that a productivity tool's value
    and the buying decision sit at senior levels, and the team agreed.
  - The delivery manager I interviewed produced the team-licence and
    security-review findings (trace T4). Those moved the pricing and the
    roadmap.
- **D22 · The code lives in a company GitHub account** (13 Sep). I cited a
  compliance warning on an earlier project; this avoided the same problem
  here.
- **Scoping the interview** (23 Sep). The 40+ question draft became 15, on
  the team's advice, so each interview fits in one sitting.
- **Deliverable list and parallel work** (13 Sep). I set out the packaging
  requirements (manual, install instructions, install command, DMG, and the
  ~$100/yr signing cost). I also planned marketing, interviews and weekly
  checkpoints to run in parallel with the build.
- **Agreed the one-week extension** proposed on 18 Sep ("breathing room").

**What my interviews changed** (traces T1–T6 in
[USER_TRACES.md](submission/research/USER_TRACES.md)):
- a text-only reading default and an app blocklist;
- the message destination shown on the preview card;
- the pitch leads with the preview;
- an employer-paid team pilot;
- the move away from "replace typing".

**Negative findings:** no price was asked, and one of the two users sees no
use for voice. Both are reported as findings.
